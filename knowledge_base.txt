# 3. History & Infrastructure Milestones
* Founding & Early Years: FCPC was founded on March 3, 1984. It officially opened on June 14, 1984, and offered a complete elementary program by 1985.
* Expansion: The institution started offering Special Science Classes in 2003, and various college courses between 2004 and 2006. The school officially changed its name to First City Providential College in 2008.
* Infrastructure: Significant campus developments include the FCPC Gymnasium (2013), Balagtas Hall (2016), and Blas Ople Hall (2018).
* Academic Excellence & Accreditation: The school boasts a 100% passing rate in licensure exams for Nursing and Teaching. Following the March 20–21, 2026 visit, the school holds the following PACUCOA Accreditations:
  - Level IV: BS Hospitality Management, BS Nursing, Bachelor of Secondary Education, Bachelor of Elementary Education, and BS Information Technology.
  - Level II: Bachelor of Science in Computer Science.
  - Level I: Master of Arts in Education (Ed. Management, English, Math), Master in Education (Science), Master of Arts in Nursing, Master of Science in Hospitality Management, and Master of Information Technology.
